## Where it sits

Every reviewer placed it structurally alongside Further and Pentagram, and below DixonBaxi,
Koto and Studio Dumbar for one reason: the site promises motion everywhere and, in the
preview, shows it nowhere. Stills where loops should be, grey boxes where films should be.

## What they all said would add the most

1. **Ship the film.** A real muted loop behind the headline, hover loops on all six tiles, a
   hero film on each case study. Estimated 8–10 points across Work and Visual.
2. **Write the BBC Sport and Cwmni Da case studies.** The names carry the studio; they need
   pages behind them.
3. **Host caching or a better host.** The lean front end is throttled by a 1.5–3.2 s server
   response; page caching alone moves Performance.

## Smaller notes to act on

- The hero still in the preview is the vedrí stage. On the live site the reel loop replaces
  it; if a poster is used it should be brand work, not the stage.
- Welsh: reads well overall. Proof before launch. Specific flags: "animeiddio cymdeithasol"
  is a calque; "Nid haen a ychwanegwn ar y diwedd yw symud" is stiff; discipline names,
  tile tags and journal titles stay English until translated in WPML (terms and posts).
- Case-study image pairs: portrait mockups ran very tall. Fixed in the theme (capped height).
- Journal is thin; three entries, two from 2025.

With the film in, the broadcaster case studies written and caching on, the reviewers'
estimates land the site in the low-to-mid 80s.
