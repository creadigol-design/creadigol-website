# Council review of the five directions

Eight independent reviewers (simulated personas, run as separate reviewers with no sight of
each other's answers) ranked the five home-page directions from the renders in
`design/previews/`. All five share the brand palette (lime #D1DF5F, charcoal #2E2E2E), Druk
headlines and Supply labels; they differ in layout and personality.

Industry reviewers split evenly between A, B and C (15 points each). Clients favoured B
(15), then A (13), C (12), E (11), D (9).

## What they agreed on

- **B has the strongest point of view** and is the only one peers and broadcast or sports
  clients said they would forward or screenshot. It "looks like it has been inside a gallery".
- **B's risks**: it wears vedrí's dark-and-lime wardrobe; it is empty until real loops play;
  gallery jargon (PGM, CAM, REC) tells smaller clients they are not the customer; two
  full-height loops push the range of work below the fold.
- **A is everyone's safe second**: credible, buildable, fastest to ship, but "a template with
  Druk poured in" and nothing on it says motion.
- **C's Welsh-first pairing is the one structural differentiator** in the set and the TV
  producer's first choice, but the grey English headline reads tentative and the page is
  static for a motion studio.
- **E is the most memorable idea and nobody's first choice**: the hero card covers the work,
  six autoplaying feeds are the heaviest page, and the metaphor needs explaining.
- **D reads as consumer tech**, dated fastest, and only the smallest client warmed to it.
- **Placeholders must never ship.** Two clients said bracketed titles would end their trust.

## Recommendation

Build on **B**, amended:

1. Bring the off-white in as a real third colour: charcoal for the reel and the closing call
   to action, off-white for the work, services and journal. That separates Creadigol from
   vedrí without touching the palette.
2. Let the reel play behind the headline, and pull the client strip and the project list up
   so the range shows without scrolling past two full-height loops.
3. Borrow C's Welsh-first headline pairing for the hero and navigation, and swap the gallery
   jargon for plain bilingual labels; keep the lower-third caption device on work tiles.
4. Launch only with real loops and real titles.
