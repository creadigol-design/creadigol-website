# Benchmark: how leading studios present work

The three studios named in the brief, plus five others that set the pattern. Direct page
loads were blocked from the build environment, so the notes combine public coverage with
prior knowledge of the sites; verify the finer points with a browser before we build.

## The three references

### DixonBaxi (London) — dixonbaxi.com
- **Thesis-first home.** One statement ("Brave brands change the world. We help create them.")
  over a full-bleed reel. No carousel, no service list above the fold.
- **Work is video.** Tiles autoplay muted loops; the grid is large, two-up, with generous
  space. The site's own chrome nearly disappears.
- **Case study pages are arguments, not galleries.** Short, punchy paragraphs organised
  around principles (Hulu's "One Hulu", Roblox's "Tilt"), each proven by motion. In 2026
  they went further and rewrote case studies as conversations (Creative Boom).
- **Studio voice everywhere.** "The DixonBaxi Way", Newsroom, the Be Brave book. The site
  sells a way of thinking, not a menu of services.
- **Take:** thesis headline, video tiles, principle-led case studies.

### Further, formerly DesignStudio (London / New York / Dubai) — further.group
- **Merged brand, one grid.** Three companies (DesignStudio, Analog, Pixel Artworks)
  presented as one work grid filtered by discipline (Brand, Campaign, Experience, Digital,
  Production) rather than by company.
- **Image-led, bright, editorial.** Big stills, restrained type, outcomes stated plainly
  (Airbnb "Belong Anywhere", EA SPORTS platform).
- **Studio pages per city** with people and contact, giving a global brand a local face.
- **Take:** discipline filters; a clean handover from a rename (the DesignStudio → Further
  announcement is a model for "Creadigol Design → Creadigol").

### Already Been Chewed (Texas) — alreadybeenchewed.tv
- **Reel-first, personality-heavy.** Bold 3D and motion in the hero; instantly says
  "motion studio".
- **Weaker structure.** All-caps ABOUT / CAPABILITIES pages, project pages at odd slugs
  (`/mandgo`), template-platform feel. Great work carried by a dated shell.
- **Take:** lead with motion, but do not let the CMS dictate the IA. This is the cautionary
  example: the work deserves a better container.

## Five more that set the pattern

| Studio | What they do well | Steal this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Koto (koto.com) | Work grid with hover-video, sector and discipline filters, optimistic copy | Hover loops on tiles |
| Studio Dumbar/DEPT | Motion as the brand's core; DEMO festival as a proof-of-belief | "Motion first" as a stated principle, not a service |
| Pentagram | Enormous, fast grid; filter by discipline, sector, partner; nothing else | Speed and restraint |
| ManvsMachine | Full-bleed film, minimal chrome | Case study hero as film |
| By-Kin (Awwwards SOTD 2026) | Next.js + GSAP + headless CMS, editorial type, smooth transitions | Modern stack, page transitions |

## Patterns that repeat across all of them

1. **One sentence, one reel.** The hero is a belief statement plus moving image. Services
   are lower or on a separate page.
2. **Work is the navigation.** The work index is the most visited page and the most cared
   for. Tiles are large, video-capable and filterable.
3. **Case studies follow a template.** Hero film → the ask → the idea → the system in
   motion → outcome → credits → next project. Templates are what make them easy to add.
4. **Restraint in type and colour.** One display face, one accent at most, lots of space.
   The work carries the colour.
5. **Studio pages sell belief and people**, not org charts.
6. **Fast.** Award-winning sites in this sector are static or server-rendered with
   optimised video, not page-builder WordPress.

## What Creadigol can do that none of them do

- **Bilingual by design.** A Welsh/English site is a genuine differentiator and a proof of
  the studio's positioning.
- **Broadcast heritage.** Sports-broadcast motion craft (BBC Sport, S4C) is a credible,
  specific story. Lead with it.
- **A sister studio.** vedrí (virtual production) gives Creadigol a production capability
  most branding studios of this size cannot offer.
