Rhan 2Benchmark

What the leading studios do, and what to take from each.

DixonBaxi

One belief statement over a full-bleed reel. Work tiles are muted video loops. Case studies are arguments organised around principles, and in 2026 they rewrote them as conversations. The site sells a way of thinking, not a menu.

Take: thesis headline, video tiles, principle-led case studies.

Further (formerly DesignStudio)

Three merged companies presented as one work grid, filtered by discipline rather than by company. Bright, image-led, outcomes stated plainly. The rename was handled with one clear announcement.

Take: discipline filters, and the model for "Creadigol Design is now Creadigol".

Already Been Chewed

Reel-first, huge personality, instantly reads as a motion studio. But all-caps template pages and odd project slugs show the CMS dictating the structure.

Take: lead with motion; never let the platform shape the IA.

Case study content model

Title and teitl · client · year · disciplines (Branding, Motion, Broadcast, Digital, Campaign) · sector · one-sentence summary in both languages · hero (video loop, Stream/Vimeo ID, or image) · 4:5 tile · body made of six block types only: text, full-bleed video, image, image pair, quote, stat row · credits · featured flag.

Six blocks cover every reference case study we looked at, and keep every page consistent.

Stack

LayerChoiceWhy
FrameworkAstro 5, static output, content collections, built-in i18n routingFast by default, Markdown-native, bilingual routes without plugins
EditingKeystatic (GitHub mode) at /keystaticA form that writes Markdown and media straight into the repo. No database. Works alongside editing files directly or with Claude Code
MotionGSAP or Motion One, View Transitions APIHover loops and page transitions without a heavy framework
VideoCloudflare Stream or Vimeo Pro for films; short self-hosted muted loops for tilesAdaptive playback, no YouTube chrome
HostingCloudflare PagesFree tier, global CDN, preview URL per branch, runs the editor
Forms and analyticsPages Functions to email; Plausible or Cloudflare Web AnalyticsNo third-party widgets, no cookie banner

Chosen over Webflow and Framer because the studio already runs sites from GitHub and works with Claude Code, and over WordPress because performance and craft are the point.

Adding a case study, in the browser

  1. Open /keystatic and choose New case study.
  2. Fill the form: titles, client, year, disciplines, summaries.
  3. Drop in the hero, the tile and the body media.
  4. Save. The site rebuilds in about a minute.

Adding a case study, as files

Create src/content/work/<slug>/index.md with the same fields as frontmatter, add media to the folder, push. Same result. This is the route to use with Claude Code.

Design direction

A · Paper & signal (lead)

Cool off-white ground, near-black ink, one broadcast tally-red accent. Archivo across its width axis for headlines, IBM Plex Mono for bilingual labels. The work carries the colour; the shell stays quiet.

B · Dark broadcast

Filmic, full-bleed reel behind the headline. Closest to the reference studios. Risk: reads close to vedrí's dark identity.

C · Grid, Welsh-first

Welsh and English side by side, visible grid, colour blocks. Risk: colder, less cinematic.

The palette is a proposal. If Creadigol has existing brand colours, a logo or a typeface, they replace it and the layouts stay the same.

Rebrand and migration

  • Every existing URL gets a 301 to its new home, for example /rar-rebrand-rondo-media/ to /work/rownd-a-rownd.
  • "Creadigol" replaces "Creadigol Design" in titles, schema.org Organization, Open Graph, footer and social bios in one pass.
  • A short Journal post announces the change, as Further did for DesignStudio.

Build phases, after approval

Phase 1 · 1 week

Astro scaffold, design tokens, layouts, Keystatic schema, i18n routing.

Phase 2 · 1–2 weeks

Home, Work index, case study template, Studio, Contact, Journal.

Phase 3 · studio-side

Ten case studies, translations, showreel, video pipeline. Runs in parallel.

Phase 4 · 1 week

Motion pass, performance and accessibility audit, redirects, launch.
