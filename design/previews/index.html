Five directions for the new Creadigol site.

Same content on every page so the comparison is fair. Headlines are set in the studio's own Druk Web Bold and labels in Supply, both taken from the current site. Only A keeps today's lime and charcoal; B to E are colour departures to go with the rename.

Grey blocks are video or image placeholders. Square-bracket text is a fact to fill in. The editable canvas holds the same boards.

Direction A

Evolution

Today's lime and charcoal kept, Druk headlines and Supply labels, a quieter off-white shell and a video-led work grid. Bilingual eyebrow labels throughout.

The least change of the five. Risk: may read as a refresh rather than a rebrand.

Direction B

Broadcast

Dark, full-bleed reel behind an uppercase Druk headline, lower-third captions on the work, an amber client ticker, a REC tally on every loop. The sports-broadcast heritage made literal.

Strongest personality for a motion studio. Risk: sits close to vedrí's dark identity.

Direction C

Swiss grid, Welsh-first

Pure white, a visible twelve-column grid, Welsh and English set side by side at equal weight, cobalt as the single colour. Work is an index, not a grid of tiles.

The most distinctive positioning statement. Risk: colder, less filmic.

Direction D

Kinetic colour

Violet, tangerine and mint blocks, rounded media, a pill navigation. Sections change colour like a sequence of idents.

Warmest and most approachable. Risk: reads younger than broadcast clients may expect.

Direction E · brand new

Multiview

The home page is a broadcast gallery monitor wall: every project is a live feed with a camera tag and tally light, the headline is stamped across the wall like a caption, services are channels, a timecode runs in the header.

Nothing in the benchmark looks like this, and it only makes sense for a studio with a gallery background. Risk: needs real loops from day one to work.
