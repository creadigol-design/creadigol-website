Sut rydyn ni'n gweithio/How we work

Motion isn't a layer we add at the end. It's the first question we ask.

Before a colour or a typeface, we ask how a brand should move: how it enters a screen, how it holds a pause, how it leaves. Everything else follows.

Gwasanaethau/What we do

Brand identity & strategy

Positioning, naming, identity systems and guidelines, designed to move from day one.

Motion & animation

Idents, title sequences, programme graphics, social and explainer animation from a sports-broadcast background.

Digital & web

Fast, bilingual websites and digital brand tools that carry the identity into every screen.
